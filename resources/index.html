Before You Hire

A quick word of advice

These are independent businesses — they're not part of First Team Real Estate, and Daniel can't guarantee their work or pricing. Treat this page as a trusted starting point, then do what you'd do with any hire: confirm the contractor's license and insurance (California licenses can be checked free at cslb.ca.gov), get the quote in writing, and don't pay in full up front.
