🔑 For Buyers — The Real Math

Buying your first home takes a lot less cash than most people think — here's the honest picture:

$2,400/mo rent ≈ $144,000That's five years of typical rent paid to a landlord — money that never comes back and never builds equity. Owning flips that math in your favor.
Myth: “You need 20% down”Reality: down payment assistance can cover most or all of it. Many of my buyers get in with $500–$5,000 out of pocket.
Myth: “My credit isn't good enough”Reality: programs exist for buyers with scores in the low 600s — and my loan partner offers options with assistance forgiven in as little as 3 years. Below that today? We'll map exactly what to fix.
✅ The credit checklist that actually helps • Keep card balances under ~30% of their limits
• Don't open new cards or finance a car or furniture before you buy
• Don't close your old cards
• Pull your free credit reports and dispute errors
• Put every bill on autopay — one 30-day late can cost you
