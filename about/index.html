From Inglewood — and working both sides of the hill

I'm from Inglewood, California — it's home, it's family, and it's where I learned what a house actually means to the people who live in it. I still serve Inglewood and the South Bay every week, because that's where I'm from and those are my people.

Today my office is at First Team Real Estate in Calabasas, and the San Fernando Valley is where I'm putting down roots — so I work both sides of the hill with the same standard: I'll tell you what I'd tell my own family, even when it's not what gets me the deal fastest.
